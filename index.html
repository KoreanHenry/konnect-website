FAQ

Got questions?

Is the 1:1 Korean/international ratio really guaranteed?
+

Yes — 100%. Korean and international spots are managed completely separately. When one side fills up, it closes and moves to a waitlist. This ensures every meetup is a genuine 50:50 balance, no exceptions.

Do I need to be fluent in Korean or English?
+

Not at all! As long as you can hold a basic conversation in one of the two languages, you'll fit right in. Our activities are designed to be inclusive of all levels.

What happens at a typical meetup?
+

You'll arrive at our cozy lounge, get matched with small groups, and rotate through fun activities — icebreakers, themed conversations, and language games. It's structured but feels totally natural.

What if I come alone?
+

80% of our members come solo! The whole format is designed to help solo attendees feel comfortable and make friends quickly. You won't be sitting awkwardly alone — promise.

Can I get a refund if I can't attend?
+

Yes. Full refund up to 7 days before, 50% refund 3-6 days before, no refund within 48 hours. See our refund policy for details.

Where exactly is the venue?
+

Our regular venue is a private lounge near City Hall Station (시청역), about 5 minutes walk from Exit 6. Exact address will be sent to confirmed attendees the day before.

Is there food or drinks?
+

Light snacks and drinks (water, tea) are provided. Many groups grab dinner together after the meetup at nearby restaurants — totally optional but a great way to keep the vibe going.

How old are most members?
+

Most of our community is in their 20s to early 30s, though we welcome anyone 18+. The vibe is young, friendly, and open-minded.

How can I contact you?
+

Easiest way is Instagram DM at @konnect_exchange or email kozykorean.official@gmail.com. We usually respond within a few hours.
